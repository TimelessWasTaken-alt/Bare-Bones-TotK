02 / The laws of the run

Universal rules

These restrictions apply in every mode unless a specific exception is written into the rule.

01Armor restriction

The only normally permitted armor is the Well-Worn Hairband, which provides no defense.

  • Water Temple exception: Zora Armor chestpiece only, when required to complete the temple. Remove it after completion. No other Zora armor pieces.
  • Fire Temple exception: Flamebreaker Helm only in the permitted Fire Temple quest area and during the allowed quest section. Do not wear it before entering or after that section is complete.
  • No other armor exceptions.
02No bonus hearts from food

Foods that grant temporary bonus hearts are prohibited. Ordinary healing food is allowed if it grants no bonus hearts. See Rule 7 for full-heal items.

03Equipment carry limit

Carry and use one permitted melee weapon, one permitted bow, and one permitted shield. Discard the current item before replacing it. No backup equipment. The Master Sword exception does not change bow or shield limits.

04No Fuse

Fuse is prohibited in every mode, with no unlock conditions. Already-fused weapons or shields found in the game may be used. Attaching an item to Mineru is allowed and does not count as using Fuse.

05No paraglider outside shrines and dungeons

The paraglider is never allowed outside shrines and dungeons. It may be used inside them when needed to progress or complete them. There are no unlock conditions; defeating Lynels or Gleeoks changes nothing.

06No Zonai devices outside shrines and dungeons

Do not use Zonai devices outside shrines and dungeons, including for device-based travel or construction.

07Food, elixirs, and temporary effects
  • You may not cook meals or brew elixirs yourself to create effects.
  • Food and elixirs obtained with effects already applied may be used if otherwise compliant.
  • Level 3 effects and bonus-heart food are prohibited.
  • Food that restores all hearts, or any item that fully restores health, may only be consumed when already at full health.
  • Wanderer allowance: 3 uses of effect-granting food/elixirs. After all 3 are spent, complete 3 additional regions to replenish the allowance to 3. A region counts only after all its applicable requirements are complete. No summit bonuses.
08No save-scumming

Do not reload older saves to undo mistakes, recover resources, reverse outcomes, or avoid consequences. Only these three cases allow loading a save from before the incident:

  1. Unavoidable accidental fight death: you accidentally enter a fight and are killed before a reasonable chance to fight, defend, or escape.
  2. Accidental fall into the Depths: you unintentionally fall in and the incident causes unavoidable death or leaves you unable to reasonably continue.
  3. Game softlock: the game state prevents reasonable continuation.

Resume without penalty only for these cases. Ordinary falls, environmental hazards, and deaths from risks deliberately taken do not qualify.

09Accidental rule breaks

If you accidentally break a rule, continue without restarting, punishment, or guilt. This is not permission to reload a save to undo the mistake; Rule 8 lists the only reload exceptions.

10Teleportation restriction

The only permitted teleport destination is the Room of Awakening, and only occasionally. All other fast travel is prohibited, including Skyview Towers, shrines, dungeons, and Travel Medallions. No summit grants extra teleport privileges.

11No intentional glitches or exploits

No intentional glitches, duplication exploits, clipping, or out-of-bounds routes. If a glitch happens accidentally, do not automatically restart; continue fairly without exploiting the result.

12Master Sword exception

In modes where the exception applies, the Master Sword may be used in addition to one chosen permitted melee weapon. No other melee weapons beyond those two. Bow and shield limits remain unchanged. In Late-Start Mode, an already-owned Master Sword stays unusable until the Late-Start unlock requirement is met. Hardcore Mode does not guarantee that you can obtain it.

13Amiibo-associated equipment ban

Weapons, bows, and shields associated with amiibo rewards are prohibited regardless of source. Examples include the Dusk Bow and White Sword of the Sky. Items already owned at the start cannot be used and do not count as permitted gear.

14Hylian Shield unlock

If you already own the Hylian Shield at challenge start, defeat 3 Stalnoxes before using it. It counts as your one permitted shield. You may use another permitted shield while working toward the unlock; discard it before switching to the Hylian Shield.

15Never back down from a fight you intentionally start

If you deliberately initiate a fight, continue until it is resolved rather than retreating because it gets difficult. If an enemy attacks unexpectedly or you accidentally attract attention, you may escape. The accidental-fight-death exception applies only if the fight was accidental and Link was killed before a reasonable chance to react or escape. Do not punish yourself over an honest mistake.

04 / Optional modifier

Hardcore mode

Hardcore can be combined with any main mode.

HARDCORE
  1. On a fresh save, start with 3 hearts.
  2. Before leaving Great Sky Island, upgrade to at least 4 hearts. This one required upgrade is permitted.
  3. After leaving Great Sky Island, no more heart upgrades.
  4. No stamina or Energy Cell upgrades at any point, including on Great Sky Island.
The Sage of Spirit problem

The quest has a 10-heart requirement, but no extra exception is granted. If you cannot meet it without breaking the rules, you cannot complete that quest under these rules.

Want the Master Sword? Sucks to be you.
Want the Sage of Spirit? Also sucks to be you.

Late-Start note: existing upgrades remain, but cannot be increased. The 4-heart departure rule is specifically for a fresh run starting on Great Sky Island; it does not automatically apply to a Late-Start run that begins elsewhere.

07 / Regional endurance

Wanderer mode

A region is not complete just because you entered it.

Core requirements

  1. Visit every required region.
  2. Survive at least 60 uninterrupted real-life minutes in each region.
  3. Explore at least 50% of each Surface region and Great Sky Island.
  4. Reach that region’s designated destination before leaving.
  5. Leaving early resets that region’s timer to zero; returning starts a fresh 60-minute timer.
  6. Explore actively; do not camp near a boundary or in a tiny area.
  7. The Depths are exempt from 50% exploration only. Other applicable requirements remain.
  8. Only occasional teleportation to the Room of Awakening is allowed.
  9. Start with 3 effect-food/elixir uses. After spending all 3, complete 3 additional regions to replenish.
  10. Summits are objectives, not rewards. No bonuses or extra teleport privileges.

Rules of the route

  • Reach each landmark during that region’s visit.
  • Do not treat an omitted region as exempt.
  • Rito Village, Eventide Island, and some regional boundaries still need confirmation.
  • The current destinations are a working list, not a complete map of every TOTK region.
