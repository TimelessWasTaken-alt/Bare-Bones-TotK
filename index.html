1. Choose your mode

Every mode follows the universal rules unless explicitly stated otherwise. Hardcore Mode can be combined with any main mode.

🌿 Survival Mode

  • Fresh save.
  • Survive as long as possible.
  • Death ends the run except for the three narrow Rule 8 recovery cases.

⚔️ Speedrun Mode

  • Fresh save.
  • Defeat Ganondorf and complete the final sequence as quickly as possible.
  • Death does not automatically end this mode.

🧭 Late-Start Mode

  • Existing save; retain progress and upgrades.
  • Start at Room of Awakening or Shrine of Resurrection.
  • Follow special setup rules below.

🏞️ Wanderer Mode

  • Visit required regions and destinations.
  • Survive 60 uninterrupted real-life minutes per region.
  • Explore at least 50% of each Surface region and Great Sky Island.
  • Leaving early resets that region’s timer.

2. Universal rules

1 — Armor restriction

Only the Well-Worn Hairband is normally permitted. Water Temple: Zora Armor chestpiece only when required to complete the temple; remove afterward. No other Zora pieces. Fire Temple: Flamebreaker Helm only in the permitted quest area and during the allowed quest portion. No other armor exceptions.

2 — No bonus hearts from food

Foods granting temporary bonus hearts are prohibited. Ordinary healing food is allowed if it grants no bonus hearts. See Rule 7 for full-heal items.

3 — Equipment carry limit

One permitted melee weapon, one permitted bow, and one permitted shield. Discard the current item before replacing it. No backups. The Master Sword exception does not change bow or shield limits.

4 — No Fuse

Fuse is prohibited in every mode, with no unlock conditions. Already-fused weapons/shields may be used. Attaching an item to Mineru is allowed and does not count as using Fuse.

5 — No paraglider outside shrines and dungeons

The paraglider is never allowed outside shrines and dungeons. It may be used inside them when needed to progress or complete them. No unlock conditions; defeating Lynels or Gleeoks changes nothing.

6 — No Zonai devices outside shrines and dungeons

Do not use Zonai devices outside shrines and dungeons, including for travel or construction.

7 — Food, elixirs, and effects

You may not cook meals or brew elixirs yourself to create effects. Effect foods/elixirs obtained already prepared may be used if otherwise compliant. Level 3 effects and bonus-heart food are prohibited. Food that restores all hearts, or any item that fully restores health, may only be consumed at full health. Wanderer allowance: 3 effect-food/elixir uses; after all are spent, complete 3 additional regions to replenish to 3. A region counts only when all applicable requirements are complete. No summit bonuses.

8 — No save-scumming

Do not reload older saves to undo mistakes, recover resources, reverse outcomes, or avoid consequences. Only these three cases allow loading a save from before the incident:

  1. Unavoidable accidental fight death: fight was entered accidentally and Link died before a reasonable chance to fight, defend, or escape.
  2. Accidental fall into the Depths that causes unavoidable death or leaves you unable to reasonably continue.
  3. Game softlock preventing reasonable continuation.

Resume without penalty only for these cases. Ordinary falls, environmental hazards, and deaths from deliberately taken risks do not qualify.

9 — Accidental rule breaks

Continue after an honest accidental rule break; no restart, punishment, or guilt. This is not permission to reload to undo a mistake. Rule 8 lists the only reload exceptions.

10 — Teleportation

Only the Room of Awakening is a permitted teleport destination, and only occasionally. All other fast travel is prohibited, including Skyview Towers, shrines, dungeons, and Travel Medallions. No summit grants extra teleport privileges.

11 — No intentional glitches or exploits

No intentional glitches, duplication, clipping, or out-of-bounds routes. If a glitch happens accidentally, do not automatically restart; continue fairly without exploiting the result.

12 — Master Sword exception

In modes where allowed, the Master Sword may be used in addition to one chosen permitted melee weapon. No other melee weapons beyond those two. Bow/shield limits remain. In Late-Start Mode, an already-owned Master Sword stays unusable until its unlock requirement is met. Hardcore does not guarantee you can obtain it.

13 — Amiibo-associated equipment ban

Weapons, bows, and shields associated with amiibo rewards are prohibited regardless of source. Examples: Dusk Bow and White Sword of the Sky. Items already owned at the start cannot be used and do not count as permitted gear.

14 — Hylian Shield unlock

If you own the Hylian Shield at challenge start, defeat 3 Stalnoxes before using it. It counts as your one shield. You may use another permitted shield meanwhile; discard it before switching to the Hylian Shield.

15 — Never back down from a fight you intentionally start

If you deliberately initiate a fight, continue until resolved rather than retreating because it gets difficult. If attacked unexpectedly or you accidentally attract attention, escape is allowed. Accidental-fight-death recovery applies only if the fight was accidental and Link died before a reasonable chance to react or escape. Do not punish yourself over an honest mistake.

4. Optional Hardcore Mode

  1. Fresh save starts with 3 hearts.
  2. Before leaving Great Sky Island, upgrade to at least 4 hearts. This required upgrade is permitted.
  3. After leaving Great Sky Island, no more heart upgrades.
  4. No stamina or Energy Cell upgrades at any point, including on Great Sky Island.
Want the Master Sword? Sucks to be you.
Want the Sage of Spirit? Also sucks to be you.

The Sage of Spirit quest has a 10-heart requirement, but no extra exception is granted. If you cannot meet it without breaking the rules, you cannot complete that quest under the current rules.

Late-Start note: existing upgrades remain but cannot be increased. The 4-heart departure rule is for a fresh run starting on Great Sky Island; it does not automatically apply to Late-Start runs beginning elsewhere.

9. Wanderer Mode

Core requirements

  1. Visit each required region.
  2. Survive 60 uninterrupted real-life minutes in each.
  3. Explore at least 50% of each Surface region and Great Sky Island.
  4. Reach the designated destination before leaving.
  5. Leaving early resets that region’s timer to zero; return starts a fresh timer.
  6. Explore actively, not by camping near a border.
  7. Depths are exempt from 50% exploration only; other requirements remain.
  8. Only occasional teleport to Room of Awakening is allowed.
  9. After spending all 3 effect-food/elixir uses, complete 3 additional regions to replenish.
  10. Summits are objectives, not rewards. No bonuses or extra teleport privileges.

Regional notes

  • Destinations must be reached during that region’s visit.
  • Do not treat omitted regions as exempt.
  • Rito Village, Eventide Island, and some regional boundaries still need confirmation.

11. Final challenge principles

  • Restrictions are real: do not invent exceptions when a rule becomes inconvenient.
  • Accidents are not moral failures: continue fairly after honest mistakes.
  • No save-scumming: accept consequences except for the three stated recovery cases.
  • No free upgrades: Hardcore’s Great Sky Island heart upgrade is the only special heart exception.
  • No guaranteed legendary gear: the Master Sword may be unavailable if requirements cannot be met legally.
  • Wanderer means wandering: complete time, exploration, and destination requirements before leaving.
  • No summit bonuses: destinations are objectives, not rewards.
  • Keep the run honest: the goal is to test yourself, not find loopholes.
You chose the restrictions. Hyrule owes you nothing.
